Method

  1. Seven indicators are computed daily from FRED data (S&P 500, VIX, VIX3M, ICE BofA HY OAS, 10y Treasury yield, Chicago Fed NFCI).
  2. Each indicator is scored 0-100 as its percentile rank versus its own trailing ~2 years (504 trading days). Indicators where a higher reading means stress (VIX, VIX/VIX3M, HY spread, NFCI) are inverted, so 100 always means greed.
  3. The composite is the equal-weight average of the available component scores (at least 5 of 7 required).
  4. Bands: 0-24 Extreme Fear, 25-44 Fear, 45-55 Neutral, 56-75 Greed, 76-100 Extreme Greed.
  5. Safe haven demand uses a 10y Treasury return proxy: -8.5 x yield change + carry. Price strength uses distance from the 52-week high in place of new highs/lows breadth, which has no free history.
  6. Live estimate: during market hours a second job pulls delayed quotes (^GSPC, ^VIX, ^VIX3M, ^TNX, HYG, IEF) from CNBC, falling back to Financial Modeling Prep, Yahoo Finance and CBOE, about every 30 minutes and scores them as if they were today's close. Junk bond demand uses the HYG/IEF move as a proxy for the HY spread change; financial conditions (weekly NFCI) does not update intraday. If a non-essential quote is unavailable, that component holds its last official value (marked 'held'). If VIX3M is unavailable it is estimated from the VIX move, using VIX3M's measured sensitivity to VIX over the past 2 years (marked 'est.'). The live number is replaced by the official score after the next morning's FRED update.
  7. This is a sentiment gauge built in the style of CNN's index, not a copy of it, and the numbers will differ. Data updates once per US trading day via GitHub Actions. Not investment advice.
